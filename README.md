Topo Wars

Topo Wars is a turn based war game played on a real place. It is for strategy fans who want to fight over their own neighborhood.

Find me or a picked place turns real roads, buildings, water, and elevation into the battlefield. Setup picks map size, opponent, player count, and difficulty. Opponents can be the computer, a friend by join code, zombies, or watch mode. Units deploy with supply, move, attack, capture, and encircle. Losing the commander eliminates a side.
